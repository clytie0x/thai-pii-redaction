# Recover Thai Source Spans Before Scaling

**Prioritize field-aware recovery of structured PII into original source spans, reusing AI Guard's existing outbound scan-only mapping.** Its value is to turn inputs that are currently blocked into correctly sanitized outputs while preserving the existing release rule, CPU-friendly CRF default, masking policy, and source coordinates. This is a conditional recommendation: first establish that the mechanism causes meaningful development-set failures; abandon or narrow it if it does not. Generic normalization, hybrid detection, span merging, and provenance are already implemented locally or established elsewhere, so none supports a standalone novelty claim. The credible research contribution is a bounded Thai representation method plus evidence that it improves successful safe release without extra leakage, excessive masking, or broken restoration. Model comparisons and coverage arbitration remain controlled alternatives, not simultaneous production projects. This report synthesizes primary-source research available on 31 October 2026; it reports **no new accuracy result**.

## Existing capabilities leave a release gap, not an empty architecture

The coordinator reports that the baseline and formatting checkpoint passed **2,749 tests with 34 skips**. Those checks are regression evidence, not a new independent estimate of Thai PII accuracy. The undisclosed heldout contains **185 documents and 714 annotations**; no inference has run on it, and this research must not open plaintext, decrypt artifacts, or score it. Preserve its freeze before tuning. The three research notes document static inspection and fabricated interval probes, not a representation benchmark ([research inputs](../research_notes/Thai%20PII%20research%20advances/privacy-methods.md)).

AI Guard already combines structured rules/checksums and Thai NER, repairs specific address/name boundaries, preserves PDF-sensitive offsets, and offers a fine-tuned model lane with overlapping token windows and development-calibrated label floors. Its leak guard already creates an obfuscation-tolerant scan view and projects detections back to source positions. The gap is narrower: the root input detector does not reuse that recovery to produce successful masking. Thus a safety scanner can identify an obfuscated residual and block release when an input-stage projection could have sanitized it. The frequency and end-to-end consequences remain unmeasured ([leak guard](../../../pii_redactor/leak_guard.py), [input aggregation](../../../pii_redactor/detectors/aggregate.py), [fine-tuned adapter](../../../pii_redactor/detectors/finetuned_engine.py)).

External work strengthens the need for an honest baseline. Presidio already supports pattern/context/validation recognition and conflict handling; its pinned resolver merges same-type intersections and offers score-based intersection removal. A new union resolver is therefore established engineering, rather than a new redaction principle ([recognizer implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/data-privacy-stack/presidio/main/presidio-analyzer/presidio_analyzer/pattern_recognizer.py), [pinned conflict implementation](https://github.com/data-privacy-stack/presidio/blob/4f0e236e31a57767958a3eec2de3fc58dffc3034/presidio-anonymizer/presidio_anonymizer/anonymizer_engine.py)). Unicode also warns that compatibility normalization can erase meaningful distinctions. Applying normalization independently to characters cannot establish whole-string equivalence or safe source alignment ([Unicode UAX #15](https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr15/)).

## Source recovery offers a stronger first experiment than generic splitting

| Option | Evidence and remaining gap | Priority decision |
|---|---|---|
| Field-aware input recovery | Existing scan-view projection supplies a reusable base; useful input masking and field isolation remain to be demonstrated. | First production candidate, conditional on a development mechanism inventory. |
| Coverage-preserving arbitration | Fabricated probes prove the greedy resolver can discard candidate-covered tails; real prevalence and true-positive benefit are unknown. | Instrument now; defer generic residual emission because restoration semantics change. |
| Existing-model constrained decoding | A fine-tuned adapter already exists; legal-transition decoding can isolate boundary/coherence errors without retraining. | Bounded research comparator if model errors dominate; retain CRF default. |
| PII Engineer ONNX | Explicit Thai claim and downloadable INT8 artifacts; published evidence mixes model and postprocessing versions. | External comparator after offset/resource validation; no replacement commitment. |

Coverage arbitration is a serious competing explanation. The current final resolver rejects an entire candidate on any overlap. In fabricated tests, TB `[0,9)` plus FP `[3,6)` retained only the FP interval, losing six candidate-covered characters; crossing TB intervals lost three. These prove a mechanical coverage loss, **not six or three leaked gold PII characters**. Earlier fallback suppression can also remove evidence before final aggregation, so replacing the final resolver alone cannot solve every loss ([core investigation](../research_notes/Thai%20PII%20research%20advances/core-gap.md)).

However, fragment emission affects the entity registry, consistency reuse, vault identity, reverse mapping, and PDF rendering. Reusing an entity ID across different residual fragments failed a fabricated token round-trip; distinct IDs restored that probe. Thai surrogate boundary rules introduce another hazard, while multiple fragments inside one PDF word can select the same entire box. Full residual splitting therefore expands scope beyond interval arithmetic. Instrument candidate coverage at each stage, but do not promote arbitrary NAME fragments to new people or treat token-mode restoration as proof of surrogate correctness. If the inventory instead shows arbitration dominates actual gold failures, revise the priority explicitly and review its representation contract separately.

The model alternative also deserves restraint. ThaiNER's official CRF card concerns Thai news NER, not redaction; PhayaThaiBERT's controlled comparisons improve ThaiNER but give mixed LST20 NER results. These establish task-dependent alternatives, not a guaranteed PII gain ([ThaiNER card](https://pythainlp.org/Model-Cards/NER/), [PhayaThaiBERT paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.12475v2)). PII Engineer advertises Thai F1 0.885, but its benchmark attributes results to a pipeline/version different from a clean v2.1 weight-only comparison. Keep that figure out of cross-system rankings ([model card](https://huggingface.co/pii-engineer/PII-Engineer-Multi-NER-v2.1), [developer methodology](https://pii.engineer/benchmarks)).

## One bounded mechanism preserves the source and existing policy

Keep the raw input immutable. Refactor or reuse the existing safety-view mapper rather than create another normalization engine. Begin with the raw view, Thai-digit canonicalization, and narrowly approved separator/format-control handling inside structured identifiers. **Never delete across a hard line or field boundary.** A candidate cannot acquire checksum validity by joining independent records. Exclude global whitespace removal, arbitrary NFKC, OCR correction, and broad Thai-name normalization from the first implementation.

Each emitted view position must carry original source provenance. Project a detected interval back to the correct raw occurrence, including removed internal separators where necessary; string search is insufficient when values repeat. Retain detector/type/provenance evidence separately from replacement coverage. Apply the existing structured detector/checksum path to the views, then pass valid source intervals through the established aggregation and anonymization path. Preserve raw baseline protection as a required invariant. Ambiguous projection or incompatible overlap must follow the existing fail-closed outcome rather than enter a new generic fragment scheme. This adds input evidence without inventing a masking policy, confidence probability, or release exception.

Validate Python code-point offsets against browser UTF-16 consumers and Thai combining marks. Original intervals must continue to select original PDF boxes; a correct string projection does not prove rendered PDF protection. Token and surrogate restoration, repeated-value consistency, and original-slice equality remain independent contract checks. Keep outbound scanning enabled: the input recovery is an additional opportunity to sanitize, not a reason to weaken the final guard.

## Measure safe release and falsify the gain before claiming accuracy

Pre-register a new visible synthetic study, separated from the undisclosed heldout. Group each seed and all perturbations in one split; separate template/document families for development, calibration where needed, and a frozen final evaluation. Freeze allowed transformations, candidate bytes, detector/model hashes, scorer, and existing release rule before evaluation. Start with a mechanism inventory covering Thai/ASCII digits, checksum-preserving separators, zero-width insertions, repeated values, mixed-script contexts, adjacent fields, invalid checksums, benign quantities, and PDF/source-coordinate cases. Gold spans must come from construction and reviewed annotations, not the detector under evaluation.

**Primary hypothesis:** source-projected recovery raises the fraction of eligible inputs that are both automatically released and completely protected. Report automatic release coverage and conditional residual leakage separately, so rejecting hard inputs cannot masquerade as better sanitization. Complete protection means every policy-required original character is covered; repeated-mention entity protection additionally requires annotated identity, not identical strings. TAB supplies precedent for complete-mention evaluation, but its English assumptions should not be imported unchanged ([TAB paper](https://aclanthology.org/2022.cl-4.19/), [released evaluator](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NorskRegnesentral/text-anonymization-benchmark/master/evaluation.py)).

Compare the frozen raw pipeline, a simple-cleaning baseline with explicit remapping, and the full proposed method; ablate each recovery view and field-boundary protection. Unsafe no-provenance cleaning belongs only in an offline diagnostic. Isolate arbitration separately by feeding identical candidate spans to the current and pinned Presidio resolvers. Report historical F2 alongside character recall, exact typed-span metrics, complete protection, unnecessary masking of gold non-PII characters, and a predefined task-utility check such as preservation of a non-sensitive amount/action/deadline. A date's policy status must be annotated rather than assumed.

The proposed advancement gates are a positive paired safe-release difference with uncertainty reported by seed/document family; no baseline protected-character loss; no new observed release leak; at most **one percentage point** extra masking of gold non-PII characters; exact required restoration; and valid original-coordinate mapping. These are targets, not results. Require an independently specified leakage non-inferiority margin and adequate sample size before making a non-inferiority claim. Zero events alone does not prove safety. Apply the repository performance budget of **20% time and 15% resident-memory overhead**, reporting cold/warm p50/p95 and peak RSS under matched conditions. Failure of mapping, field isolation, restoration, utility, or performance blocks advancement even if F2 increases.

Metamorphic relations strengthen falsification: equivalent valid identifiers should retain complete source protection after allowed representation changes; candidate-order permutation must preserve coverage; adding accepted evidence must not uncover protected characters; text outside the replacement ledger must remain unchanged. These methods have NER and behavioral-testing precedent, and agreement can still occur when both outputs are wrong ([NER metamorphic study](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/sfw2.12058), [CheckList](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.442/)).

## Conclusion

The immediate difficult work is to demonstrate a useful causal improvement across **representation, source projection, and release**, rather than add another detector. If the inventory or frozen evaluation rejects that improvement, retain the negative result and avoid a novelty claim. Explicit-span protection also cannot establish anonymity or resistance to linkage attacks; attacker knowledge and resources matter independently of annotation recall ([TRIA/TRIR study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10618-024-01066-3)).

After that decision, bound any comparator to one fixed checkpoint and visible synthetic families. Existing-model legal-transition decoding can test boundary coherence with unchanged weights, an established approach illustrated by Privacy Filter ([first-party implementation](https://github.com/openai/privacy-filter)). PII Engineer can test complementary Thai errors after offset and CPU/RAM checks, with raw-model and postprocessing results separated. Neither justifies heavyweight default inference, private upload, paid APIs, deployment, or opening the frozen heldout. A defensible YRSS claim would describe measured gains on registered Thai representation families and their limits; general Thai superiority still requires independent, fresh human-annotated evidence.
