# Privacy methods: source-aligned Thai redaction and release evaluation

Research date and cutoff: 2026-10-31. Scope: public primary sources and the ordinary development checkout only. No external holdout directory, blind document, decrypted artifact, paid provider, private upload, or product execution was accessed. This is a research proposal, not a measured improvement or proof of anonymity.

## 1. What already exists, and where is a defensible remaining gap?

### Takeaway

Hybrid regex/checksum/NER recognition, overlap merging, replacement operators, and privacy-versus-utility evaluation are established. AI Guard already has source-mapped obfuscation recovery in its outbound safety scanner. A defensible local direction is to extend that mechanism into **successful input detection and masking**, with hard field boundaries and measured utility. The research contribution would be the extension, Thai threat model, and reproducible evidence together; this search does not establish a world-first algorithm.

### Cited Findings

- **Presidio recognition is already extensible.** `PatternRecognizer` accepts regex, deny lists, context, and checksum validation hooks; successful validation can raise a score to the recognizer maximum. `EntityRecognizer` supports context enhancement and per-entity thresholds. Consequently, adding another regex, a checksum, context boosting, or an ensemble is weak architectural novelty. Sources: [pattern recognizer source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/data-privacy-stack/presidio/main/presidio-analyzer/presidio_analyzer/pattern_recognizer.py), [entity recognizer source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/data-privacy-stack/presidio/main/presidio-analyzer/presidio_analyzer/entity_recognizer.py), [official extension tutorial](https://presidio.dataprivacystack.org/analyzer/adding_recognizers/).
- **Presidio conflict resolution already handles important overlap cases.** At commit `4f0e236e31a57767958a3eec2de3fc58dffc3034` (file's latest commit returned by GitHub API, 2026-10-19), the default is `MERGE_SIMILAR_OR_CONTAINED`; same-type intersections become their union, contained results are dropped, equal intervals retain the higher score, and `REMOVE_INTERSECTIONS` trims crossings according to scores. Adjacent same-type entities separated by spaces can merge. Source: [pinned anonymizer implementation](https://github.com/data-privacy-stack/presidio/blob/4f0e236e31a57767958a3eec2de3fc58dffc3034/presidio-anonymizer/presidio_anonymizer/anonymizer_engine.py). Documentation describes larger-span containment and concatenated replacements for partial intersections: [official anonymizer docs](https://presidio.dataprivacystack.org/anonymizer/). These are baseline capabilities, not proposed contributions. The legacy Microsoft docs redirect through the current project domain; pin source bytes for experiments.
- **Normalization has nontrivial semantics.** Unicode explicitly warns against blindly applying NFKC/NFKD because compatibility distinctions can matter; normalized strings are not generally closed under concatenation. Source: [Unicode UAX #15](https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr15/). Per-code-point normalization followed by concatenation therefore cannot be assumed equivalent to normalizing the complete sequence.
- **Local observation, not a benchmark result:** `clean_length_preserving()` deliberately performs only Thai-digit substitution for callers holding PDF boxes, whereas `clean()` also collapses whitespace, applies NFC, and removes selected zero-width characters. `aggregate.py` has FP-first overlap selection, address coalescing, and address-tail extension. The current accuracy plan already demands union coverage for partial overlaps. Sources: [text cleaner](../../../../pii_redactor/ingest/text_cleaner.py), [aggregate](../../../../pii_redactor/detectors/aggregate.py), [current research plan](../../../../docs/research/yrss-accuracy-plan.md). These observations do not prove an exploitable failure in a current end-to-end route.
- **Additional verified repository context supplied by the coordinating researcher:** `_security_scan_view`, `_map_security_view_entities`, and `scan_obfuscated_structured_entities` already remove format controls/repeated whitespace in a scan-only view, project exact original intervals, and guard outbound/restored results. The root `detect_all` does not reuse that recovery for input masking, so some obfuscated input can be blocked instead of successfully sanitized. Source: [existing leak guard](../../../../pii_redactor/leak_guard.py). Thus generic normalization, provenance, or obfuscation scanning cannot be claimed as new local functionality.
- **Recent alternatives go beyond explicit names.** RAT-Bench (arXiv v1, 2026-02-13; preprint status) constructs synthetic texts using U.S. demographic statistics and evaluates direct/indirect identifiers through an LLM attacker. Its multilingual experiments are English, Spanish, and Simplified Chinese, not Thai. It reports unusual identifier forms and combinations of indirect identifiers as failure modes. Source: [RAT-Bench v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.12806v1). RUPTA instead uses LLM privacy evaluation, utility evaluation, and optimization, and investigates distillation: [ACL 2025 paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1404/).

### Inferences

- A general-purpose LLM anonymizer adds cost, deployment complexity, and another potential disclosure boundary. Under the available CPU/no-paid-provider constraint, first study a bounded representation method using the existing recognizers. This is a resource-based selection, not evidence that the lightweight method beats RUPTA or RAT-Bench systems.
- The material gap is **turning existing source-mapped safety detection into useful input masking**, especially across hard fields/lines and coordinate-preserving PDF processing. “Normalize Thai text,” “retain provenance,” and “merge spans” are already present. The proposed extension must demonstrate more successful sanitization than today's block-only safety outcome without unsafe field joining or coordinate drift.
- Keep semantic label selection separate from the mask coverage ledger. A checksum-valid identifier can retain its honest type while an overlapping uncertain tail is masked or routed to review without being falsely relabeled. Generic union alone is neither novel nor sufficient to establish safety.

### Gaps

- No current measured failure rate for the proposed representation classes was collected. Start with a development-only mechanism inventory; abandon or narrow the idea if the gap is negligible.
- No evidence here establishes complete Thai support or unsupported status for every Presidio recognizer/model combination. A fair Thai baseline needs the same structured recognizers and a documented Thai NER adapter, rather than an English default.
- This bounded search did not establish that source-aligned normalization is new to all text redaction systems. A publication novelty claim needs a broader related-work check.

## 2. Which evaluation and testing methods make the work scientifically stronger?

### Takeaway

Keep historical F2, character coverage, and boundary results, then add output-level complete protection and task utility. Treat metamorphic tests as a mechanism probe and falsification tool; they cannot establish accuracy when both source and transformed outputs are wrong.

### Cited Findings

- **TAB is privacy-oriented rather than generic NER.** Its 1,268 English ECHR cases annotate masking decisions, direct/quasi-identifiers, confidential attributes, and co-reference. It evaluates privacy and utility. Source: [original TAB paper](https://aclanthology.org/2022.cl-4.19/). Its released evaluator counts an entity protected only when all relevant mentions are masked, and supports information-weighted precision. It also ignores certain punctuation/tokens and uses English NLP assumptions. Source: [TAB evaluator](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NorskRegnesentral/text-anonymization-benchmark/master/evaluation.py). Borrow the all-mentions principle, not the English skip lists or BERT weighting without Thai validation.
- **Recall is not re-identification risk.** Manzanares-Salor, Sánchez, and Lison show that annotation completeness is an inadequate substitute for risk and propose TRIA/TRIR, an attack-based linkage/classification framework whose outcome depends on attacker background breadth, depth, and resources. Source: [original 2024 study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10618-024-01066-3). An unsuccessful attack does not prove anonymity against stronger attackers.
- **Metamorphic NER testing has primary precedent.** Xu et al. (2022) define expected relations across related executions, test name-extension and entity-swap relations on industrial NER, and investigate violations. Some violations originate from wrong source predictions, illustrating why a relation violation is diagnostic evidence rather than a ready-made gold label. Source: [original study](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/sfw2.12058).
- **Behavioral testing itself is established.** CheckList provides capability-oriented NLP behavioral tests, beyond one aggregate accuracy number. Source: [original ACL 2020 paper](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.442/). A collection of tests can support a contribution but is not, by itself, a new redaction method.
- **Selective prediction trades coverage for conditional error.** Geifman and El-Yaniv study rejecting inputs to meet a target classification risk under their statistical setup. Source: [original NeurIPS 2017 paper](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/file/4a8423d5e91fda00bb7e46540e2b0cf1-Paper.pdf). Their neural classification guarantees do not automatically apply to heuristic redaction scores or detector disagreement.

### Inferences

The following are proposed operational definitions, not literature-reported AI Guard results:

- **Complete document protection:** fraction of released documents in which every policy-required original character is protected. Also report complete entity protection across repeated mentions if independently annotated entity IDs exist. Do not infer co-reference merely from identical strings.
- **Automatic release coverage:** released documents / eligible input documents. **Conditional residual leak rate:** released documents with any required unprotected source content / released documents. Report blocked/reviewed documents separately; calling rejection a correctly redacted output hides usability cost.
- **Utility:** unnecessary masked characters outside gold spans; exact preservation outside the coverage ledger; and a small, predefined downstream task, such as retaining a document's requested action, amount, and non-sensitive deadline. Annotate which task fields must survive; a date can itself be identifying. Precision is an edit-cost proxy, not complete task utility.
- **Repeated detector recheck:** a useful consistency control, but shares detector blind spots. An independent character-origin ledger can verify that asserted spans were replaced without asserting that every PII span was detected.
- **Metamorphic relations with preconditions:** use same-valid-value Thai/ASCII digit conversion, allowed separators inside structured identifiers, controlled zero-width insertions, and repeated-mention placement. Preserve checksum validity. Require all designated original characters to be covered after inverse alignment; do not demand identical offsets after a length change. Entity swaps need agreement that the role and policy remain the same. Whitespace within arbitrary Thai names or across words is not automatically meaning-preserving.
- **Pipeline properties:** candidate order permutation must not change protected character coverage; adding an accepted mask candidate must not uncover previous characters; mapped spans must be in bounds; source outside the final ledger must remain unchanged. Check these with randomized synthetic intervals and deterministic seeds. A Hypothesis-style development dependency is optional; no new production dependency is needed for these checks.
- **Uncertainty:** confidence/disagreement may rank review priority, but call it an operational score until calibrated on an independent calibration set. Show the full release-coverage/error curve. Never label a heuristic threshold a formal probability guarantee.
- **Finite-sample interpretation:** for a frozen release rule, zero leaks in `n` independent representative released documents yields the one-sided binomial 95% upper bound `1 - 0.05^(1/n)`, approximately `3/n`. At `n=300`, this is about 0.994%, not zero risk. This is a mathematical calculation; grouped templates, synthetic sampling, and distribution shift weaken its population interpretation.

### Gaps

- Existing inline spans alone do not supply entity identity, attacker background, or task utility labels. These require a small separately specified annotation extension.
- A CPU lexical retrieval attacker on a wholly synthetic population is locally feasible, but demonstrates only that toy attacker/population. It cannot reproduce TRIA's strength or estimate Thai population re-identification risk. Keep it an exploratory stress test if used.

## 3. What ambitious local improvement should be preregistered, and how can it fail?

### Takeaway

Build one bounded extension: **reuse source-aligned safety views for input masking, with field-aware boundaries and fail-closed projection**. Compare it against the frozen current pipeline and simple cleaning. The key objective is higher successful release coverage at equal or lower residual leakage and bounded over-masking, rather than merely replacing a correct block with a risky release. No numerical gain is claimed yet.

### Cited Findings

- The existing scan-only projection and offset-preserving PDF cleaner establish the local starting point. Established external alternatives include Presidio overlap handling, TAB complete-mention evaluation, Unicode normalization constraints, and NER metamorphic testing. Sources: [leak guard](../../../../pii_redactor/leak_guard.py), [local cleaner](../../../../pii_redactor/ingest/text_cleaner.py), [pinned Presidio code](https://github.com/data-privacy-stack/presidio/blob/4f0e236e31a57767958a3eec2de3fc58dffc3034/presidio-anonymizer/presidio_anonymizer/anonymizer_engine.py), [TAB evaluator](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NorskRegnesentral/text-anonymization-benchmark/master/evaluation.py), [Unicode UAX #15](https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr15/), [NER metamorphic study](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/sfw2.12058).

### Inferences

**Proposed mechanism.** Extract/reuse the existing safety view and mapping rather than implement a second normalization engine. Keep raw input immutable. Create a small fixed set of detection views: raw text; Thai-digit canonicalization; and a strictly scoped structured-identifier view with approved separator/zero-width handling. Preserve hard line and field boundaries so deletion cannot manufacture an identifier across records. Each view retains an explicit provenance relation from normalized positions to original source intervals, including deleted characters between emitted characters. This relation supports projection, not mathematical invertibility of lossy normalization. Detect on the views using the existing regex/checksum path; optionally apply CRF only where development evidence justifies extra runtime. Project candidates to raw intervals, preserve type/provenance separately, combine protection coverage deterministically, and replace in original text. Invalid/ambiguous provenance or failed NER chunks lead to reject/review. PDF coordinates always refer to original intervals.

Do not begin with arbitrary full NFKC, OCR spelling correction, or global whitespace deletion. These change semantics or join unrelated numbers. If later added, they require their own alignment algorithm, policy, and negative controls. A digit-only view can be implemented with ordinary Python and the current dependencies. General canonical-composition alignment is harder because normalization can reorder or compose sequences; independently normalizing each character is invalid.

**Proposed falsifiable primary hypothesis.** On a newly frozen synthetic Thai representation challenge, reusing source-aligned views for input masking increases successful automatic release coverage over the unchanged current pipeline while not increasing conditional residual leakage or reducing raw baseline protection, with no more than 1 percentage point extra unnecessary-character masking and no more than 20% median runtime increase. These are proposed targets, not validated limits. Pre-register a non-inferiority margin for residual leakage using an attainable sample size; an underpowered zero-event result cannot establish equivalence. If the gap is small or compute target fails, report the negative result and retain a narrower structured-only method. Include p95 latency and peak RSS under matched warm/cold conditions.

**Required comparisons and ablations.** A: frozen current raw pipeline. B: existing generic cleaning before detection, evaluated with explicit alignment consequences. C: the same approved views without provenance-aware protection, an offline diagnostic only. D: full proposed method. Ablate each view and the coverage/review policy. Feed identical gold candidate spans into current versus pinned Presidio conflict resolution to isolate the resolver; separately compare complete systems with matched Thai recognition. Do not confound recognizer quality with replacement correctness. Keep a wide destructive union/mask-everything control as an upper-protection/lower-utility reference, not a deployable recommendation.

**Failure paths to measure.** Non-PII quantities or adjacent records joined into identifiers; invalid checksum strings converted into false positives; valid identifier tails left uncovered by crossing spans; deleted invisible characters assigned to the wrong side; combining marks or compatibility characters shifted; Python code-point versus browser UTF-16 offsets; repeated identical strings at different positions; benign text unnecessarily masked by a union; missing PDF word boxes, lines, or ligatures; fallback after a failed mapping; strong apparent results created solely by rejecting hard documents. Residual semantic or quasi-identifier disclosure remains outside explicit-span guarantees.

**Leakage prevention.** The current campaign's blind corpus must remain untouched. Author this study as a new protocol: publish allowed transformations and metrics first; keep development families and independent evaluation families separate by template/document provenance; group all perturbations of the same seed in one split; reserve calibration documents for any threshold; pin detector/model/dependency/scorer/view-generator bytes. Freeze one candidate and one release rule before aggregate evaluation. Bootstrap paired differences by original document or seed family, not by correlated transformed examples. After a final reveal, fixes belong to development and need a fresh future holdout. If a transformation family is withheld to test extrapolation, document that treatment and report it separately. Never tune on published evaluation examples and then describe them as unseen.

**Honest eventual claim.** “On the registered synthetic Thai representation families, the method improved complete source-span protection under the stated utility and compute constraints.” It would not establish anonymity, PDPA compliance, resistance to all inference attacks, physical-scan correctness, production safety, or general Thai population risk.

### Gaps

- No prototype, property execution, statistical evaluation, CPU measurement, or PDF visual verification was performed in this research subtask. Product code was intentionally not edited.
- All primary-paper results are from their own settings. None supplies a transferable numerical gain for AI Guard. The proposed thresholds and mechanism must be tested and can be rejected.
